The Machine

Heads-up poker against a bot that shows the real math behind every decision — and structurally cannot see your cards. This is the one that survives a skeptic.

  1. Hit Verify the math. It deals every one of 1,712,304 possible boards, live, and lands on 81.9461%. Google "AA vs KK equity" while it runs.
  2. Open What the bot actually sees — its literal input. Your cards are not in it. The header counts every decision it has audited.
  3. Play a few hands. Raise big once and watch the grid of your possible hands collapse from 1,326 down.
  4. Fold to one of its bets. It immediately reveals whether it had you beaten or was bluffing — with a hash proving it didn't rewrite the story after.
  5. Hit Let the bot cheat. Same bot, now fed your real cards. It behaves completely differently.
The momentIt starts each hand assuming you could hold any two cards, then narrows that purely from how you bet. Watching the grid go dark is the part people don't have an explanation for.

Rate My Parlay

Real sportsbook math on a bet slip he types in himself. Fast, personal, and impossible to write off as a canned demo.

  1. Hand him the screen. Have him enter a real parlay — his actual legs, his actual odds.
  2. Read the true win probability out loud before he sees it.
  3. Add one more leg and watch the Sucker Meter climb.
The momentIt converts American odds to true implied probability and exact payout — so the number is his bet, not a demo bet. The roast escalates with how deranged the slip actually is.
✓Implied probability from American odds; combined across legs
✓Payout exact to the cent — hand-verified: -150 + 120 on $20 → 27.3% / $73.33

Coast Clash
